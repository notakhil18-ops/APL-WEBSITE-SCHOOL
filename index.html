My experience in APL was very great and exciting. I felt very happy when I was given the opportunity to play with my teammates and classmates. From the beginning, I enjoyed playing with my friends because they made me feel comfortable and supported me. However, the experience was not always easy. Sometimes, my teammates blamed me for losing a match because of my mistakes, which made me feel sad and disappointed. Even during those difficult moments, my coaches encouraged me, helped me improve my game, and taught me to stay focused. At the beginning of the first match, I was nervous because I wanted to play well and did not want to disappoint my team. However, as soon as I started playing, I became more comfortable and began to enjoy the match. Although some of my classmates insulted me and said that I did not know how to play, I tried not to let their words affect me. Even though my team was not as strong as some of the other teams, we continued to give our best and support each other throughout every match and difficult moment. One of my favourite parts of APL was cheering for my teammates whenever they scored runs or goals. Whenever my team won, I felt extremely happy, especially when I had contributed to the victory. On the other hand, losing made me feel disappointed. Still, I learned that losing is also important because it teaches us to identify our mistakes and make sure we do not repeat them in the next match. Overall, according to me, APL is not only about playing sports. It also teaches us teamwork, cooperation, confidence, and how to enjoy winning moments. More importantly, it teaches us to learn from our losses and improve ourselves. APL has given me many unforgettable memories, and I believe it is one of the best experiences of my school life.
